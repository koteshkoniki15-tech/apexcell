Transparent Mathematical Formulations

Every output displayed in ApexCell is computed with transparent, peer-reviewed engineering equations.

Module A: Financial Savings & Carbon Reductions

  • Monthly Distance: \(D_{\text{month}} = D_{\text{daily}} \times 30\text{ days}\)
  • EV Electricity Cost: \(C_{\text{EV}} = \left(\frac{D_{\text{month}}}{\eta_{\text{EV}}}\right) \times \text{Tariff}_{(\text{₹/kWh})}\)
  • Petrol Fuel Cost: \(C_{\text{Petrol}} = \left(\frac{D_{\text{month}}}{\eta_{\text{Petrol}}}\right) \times \text{Price}_{(\text{₹/Litre})}\)
  • Payback Horizon: \(T_{\text{Payback}} = \frac{\Delta \text{Purchase Price}}{(C_{\text{Petrol}} - C_{\text{EV}}) \times 12}\text{ years}\)
  • Net Carbon Avoided: $$\text{CO}_2\text{ Avoided} = \left[\left(\frac{D}{\eta_{\text{Petrol}}} \times 2.31\text{ kg/L}\right) - \left(\frac{D}{\eta_{\text{EV}}} \times 0.716\text{ kg/kWh}\right)\right]$$ Reference: Central Electricity Authority (CEA) Baseline Database for Indian Power Grid (0.716 kg CO2/kWh).

Module B: Highway Trip, GPS Routing & Charging Stops

  • Initial Safe Leg: \(D_{\text{first}} = \text{Capacity} \times \left(\frac{\text{SoC}_{\text{start}} - \text{SoC}_{\text{reserve}}}{100}\right) \times \eta_{\text{EV}}\)
  • GPS Highway Mapping: Plots geo-referenced waypoints across major corridors (NH 48, NH 44, NH 65, Mumbai-Pune Expressway) using high-precision Leaflet polyline geometry.
  • Fast Charging Duration: $$t_{\text{charge}} = \frac{E_{\text{required}}}{P_{\text{charger}} \times \eta_{\text{charger}}} \times 60\text{ minutes}$$ Includes standard 90% AC-to-DC conversion and thermal derating efficiency factors.

Module C: Real-World Range & Degradation Risk

  • Range Multipliers: \(R_{\text{real}} = R_{\text{ideal}} \times (k_{\text{speed}} \cdot k_{\text{AC}} \cdot k_{\text{load}} \cdot k_{\text{terrain}} \cdot k_{\text{style}})\)
  • Aerodynamic Speed Drag: Evaluates exponential aerodynamic drag losses occurring above 65 km/h.
  • Degradation Risk Model: Educational matrix assessing daily charge targets (>80% voltage stress) and C-rate fast charging thermal frequency.

Transparent Scope & Platform Boundaries

ApexCell maintains clear, honest engineering boundaries.

Aspect Platform Boundary & Architectural Reality
Vehicle Telemetry & OBD-II ApexCell does not connect to OBD-II dongles, vehicle CAN-bus, or proprietary telematics APIs. All calculations rely on verified vehicle specifications and user inputs.
Live Charger Feeds Clearly distinguishes recommended stop intervals and simulated GPS hubs from live physical availability. Real-time OCPP status feeds are scheduled for enterprise integrations.
Battery Health Output Positioned as an educational risk indicator. Does not claim cell-level diagnostic warranty determinations.
Trip Durations Separates theoretical pure driving time from charging stop durations without assuming unpredictable traffic gridlocks.
