Actionable EV Care Advice

  • The 20%–80% Rule: Keep everyday battery SoC between 20% and 80%. This prevents lithium plating and thermal strain.
  • Avoid Parking at 100%: Charge to 100% only immediately before departing on long highway trips; do not leave parked at 100% under direct Indian sun.
  • LFP vs NMC Chemistry: Many Indian EVs (e.g. Tata Nexon EV, BYD) use LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) chemistry which benefits from an occasional 100% charge every 1–2 weeks for BMS cell balancing.
